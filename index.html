The Secret Beneath the Hall

A mystery, read aloud and taken apart, one chapter at a time.

A first-year student goes missing from a hall of residence in Bristol in her first week. The hall says she withdrew. Her roommate doesn’t believe it. Under the building there’s a room nobody is supposed to know about, and on its walls there’s writing that was old when Rome was young.

That’s the book. This site is where I go through it properly. Each chapter gets a video where I read the best parts, explain anything that’s easy to miss, and talk about the real history underneath it: Nineveh, the Assyrian kings, cuneiform, the oldest written music we have, and a ritual that actually existed.

I also play the ney in some of them. It matters to the story. You’ll see why.

Start with the prologue. Mosul, 1879.
